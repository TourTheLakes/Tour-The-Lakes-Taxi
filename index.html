Private Tours

Discover the Lake District entirely at your own pace with a private tour designed around you. With exclusive use of the vehicle and your own driver for the journey, you can explore the places that interest you most, spend longer where you wish, and experience the Lakes without being tied to the itinerary or timetable of a scheduled tour.

Your Day — Your Lake District

Private tours for up to 7 passengers

A private tour gives you the freedom to experience the Lake District your way. There is no fixed group to follow, no set collection of stops and no need to fit your day around somebody else’s itinerary.

Tell us what you would love to see, the places that interest you and how you would like your day to feel. Whether that means famous Lake District highlights, quieter villages, dramatic mountain scenery, time beside the lakes, or simply leaving some of the decisions to us, we will work tirelessly with you to put together a day that is personal to you.

Tailor Made

Using our knowledge of the Lake District, we will take care of the practical details behind your day. We will consider the route, travelling times, suitable stops and how best to use the time you have available, while making sure there is actually time to enjoy the places you have come to see.

Your itinerary does not have to be decided before you contact us either. Tell us as much or as little as you already know. If you have a list of places you absolutely want to visit, we can build around them. If you simply know the sort of Lake District experience you are looking for, leave the finer details to us.

And because the vehicle is exclusively yours, your day has far greater freedom than a scheduled tour. Where practical, there is room to spend longer somewhere you particularly enjoy, change the emphasis of the day, or adapt the journey as you explore.

Executive Travel

Travel through the Lake District in comfort with a dedicated executive car and driver. From hotels, restaurants and business appointments to special occasions and important journeys, our executive travel service is arranged around your timings, your destination and the standard of journey you expect.

Arrive Well

Executive travel for business, leisure and special occasions

Some journeys deserve more than simply getting from one place to another. Our BMW 7 Series executive saloon offers a quiet, comfortable and discreet way to travel through the Lake District, whether you are heading to a country house hotel, a restaurant, an event, a meeting or the next part of a longer itinerary.

The service works particularly well for individuals, couples and smaller parties who value privacy, space and a professional journey. Private Hire

Travel across the Lake District and beyond with private hire arranged around your journey. From airport and railway transfers to hotels, weddings, events, evenings out and longer distance travel, we provide comfortable transport with a dedicated driver for individuals, families and groups of up to seven passengers.

Your Journey

Private hire for up to 7 passengers

Private Hire gives you the flexibility to arrange transport around where you need to be and when you need to be there. We can cover local Lake District journeys, hotel and restaurant transfers, railway stations, airports, evenings out, family travel, events and longer distance trips beyond Cumbria.

For smaller parties we can offer executive car travel, while groups can travel together in our spacious passenger vehicle for up to seven passengers. Tell us who is travelling, what the journey involves and any timings that matter, and we will advise which option best suits the booking and availability.

Transfers And Events

When the journey is part of a bigger plan, reliability matters. We can arrange collection for railway and airport connections, hotel arrivals and departures, restaurant reservations, celebrations, concerts, days out and private events. Return travel can be planned at the same time, avoiding the need to organise another vehicle later in the day.

For visitors to the Lake District, this is also a practical option when you want to move between accommodation, attractions and onward transport without hiring a car or relying on several separate bookings.

Wedding Travel

Your wedding transport should feel calm, polished and properly planned. Tour The Lakes can arrange wedding travel across the Lake District for the couple, family members, the bridal party and guests, with an executive car available for smaller parties and private hire for groups of up to seven passengers.

Whether you need transport to a country house venue, hotel, ceremony, reception, restaurant or accommodation afterwards, we can work through the timings with you before the day. Multiple journeys, guest movements and return travel can all be discussed as part of the same enquiry, subject to vehicle availability.

If presentation, timings or a particular sequence of collections matters to you, tell us. Wedding travel is quoted individually so we can build the transport around the day you are actually planning rather than force it into a standard package.
