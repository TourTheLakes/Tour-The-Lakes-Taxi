A Taste Of The Lakes

Lunch & Distillery

At a Glance

Overview

We’ll collect you from your designated pick-up point, so you can sit back, relax and enjoy the day while we take care of the driving.

Our first stop is Forest Side, where you can delight in a Michelin Star four course lunch in beautiful Lake District surroundings.

We will then head to the scenic village of Grasmere, where you can enjoy a Distillery Taste and Tour experience, including award winning Gin, Whisky, Rum and Vodka.

  • Pickup from Ambleside, Kendal or Windermere Railway Station
  • Michelin Star four course lunch at Forest Side
  • Visit to the scenic village of Grasmere
  • Distillery Taste and Tour experience
  • Locally made Gin, Whisky, Rum and Vodka

Itinerary

Forest Side & Grasmere

We’ll collect you from your designated pick-up point and take care of the driving throughout the day. Our first stop is Forest Side for a Michelin Star four course lunch in beautiful Lake District surroundings.

We will then continue to Grasmere for a Distillery Taste and Tour experience, including award winning Gin, Whisky, Rum and Vodka, before returning you to your original pickup area.

Meeting Point

Please arrive at your selected pickup point 10 minutes before your scheduled pickup time. Your Tour The Lakes driver will meet you at the designated pickup area. Please remain at the agreed meeting point until your driver arrives.

If you have any difficulty locating your vehicle or driver, please contact Tour The Lakes for assistance. The tour returns to your original pickup area at the end of the day.

Kendal
Brewery Arts, 122A Highgate, Kendal, Cumbria LA9 4HE. Meet in the main car park by the parking meter.

Windermere Railway Station
Station Precinct, Windermere, Cumbria LA23 1AH. Face the railway station and the large yellow Pick Up bay is approximately 20 feet to the left. Your vehicle will meet you there.

Ambleside
Main Bus Interchange, King Street Car Park, Ambleside. Meet near the furthest EV charging point at the yellow Taxi pickup marked on the ground.
